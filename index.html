About PTHS

From playgroup to high school, we've got them covered

PTHS — Play To High School — began with a simple belief: getting quality uniforms to students shouldn't be a headache for schools or a burden on parents. We act as a trusted mediator between educational institutions and reliable manufacturers, cutting out inflated middleman costs while keeping quality high.

Today we serve institutions of every size, from small playgroups to large colleges, with a single promise: the right uniform, the right fit, delivered on time.
